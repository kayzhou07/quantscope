## B. Corrected data-defect analysis (supersedes the "extreme non-corporate moves" line above)

The audit above used a fixed 21% threshold, which wrongly flags legal ChiNext 20% limit days and
first-week IPO no-limit moves as anomalies. Re-run with date/board-aware bands
(main 10%, ChiNext 10% before 2020-08-24 / 20% after), excluding rows whose provider `preclose`
is the ex-rights reference price:

* 324 raw moves beyond the board band with no reference preclose, categorised as:
  * **189 legal first-5-trading-day new-listing moves** (no price limit in a new listing's first
    days; engine currently special-cases only the IPO day itself -> documented limitation;
    effects are conservative: a few orders may be blocked/carried in a name's first week).
  * **109 corporate actions where the qfq total-return factor is present** -> engine branch-2
    (`qfq_ratio`) applies the value-preserving multiplier whenever the name is held.
  * **26 residual rows** with moves of only +-11.5%..13.3% where neither the reference preclose nor
    the qfq factor captured the event (likely legal no-limit resumption / delisting-arrangement
    first days, e.g. 600401 2019-06-19 -12.5%, 300104 2020-07-03 -11.5%, 002087 2024-06-17 +12.5%).
    Residual impact is immaterial: 26 stock-days out of 12.1M (0.0002%) and each is a one-day
    level change bounded by ~13%. The engine's `inferred_missing_action` fallback (drop-only,
    m in [1.1, 3.0], persistence-checked) will preserve value on such a day if the name is held;
    this is a small optimistic bias bounded by one day's move on those rare names, disclosed here.
    After the current os/ results batch this fallback will be made opt-in
    (`infer_missing_actions=False` default) so the frozen behaviour matches the documented qfq /
    reference-preclose rules.

No evidence of missing large (20%+) splits: the 109 qfq-adjusted cases are all handled when held,
and the largest residual is 13.3%. Raw prices and qfq levels match baostock exactly on the
12-name x 4-date spot check (raw 29/29, qfq 29/29 within 0.2%).
